# Product Overview — New Tangerine Wealth

> **Source:** `Tiered Model Strategy May 26.pptx` (proposal, last updated Aug 14 2026).

## Where Wealth is going

Tangerine Wealth launched an **MVP app** and is now building a **tiered client experience** anchored in three levers:

1. **Digital Experience** — a common self-serve foundation for all clients, with tier-specific visual cues and progressively richer planning/insight tools.
2. **Advisory Model** — the primary differentiator: from team-based support up to a dedicated CFP advisor.
3. **Product Differentiation** — core products for everyone; advanced portfolio solutions, managed solutions, and tax-efficient strategies unlock as needs grow.

Tangerine Wealth remains the master brand; tiering differentiates the experience for higher-value clients and motivates asset **consolidation**.

## Roadmap phases

| Phase | Name | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wealth MVP | Build momentum; focus clients on their "full financial picture". **Done.** |
| 2 | **Foundational Experience (MVP2)** | Help clients understand their holistic picture and the value of investing better. **Current focus.** |
| 3 | Light Advice | Full investment platform (ETFs, equities), larger product shelf, enhanced capabilities. |
| 3+ | Full Offering | Trusted-partner experience with advanced capabilities. |

The three target AI use cases sit inside the **Phase 2 / MVP2 "Financial Planning"** and **"Portfolio insights"** digital-experience capabilities.

## Tiers at a glance (see [02-tiered-model-strategy.md](02-tiered-model-strategy.md) for detail)

| Tier | Name | Threshold (investments¹) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Everyday Wealth** | $25K–$250K |
| 2 | **Preferred Wealth** | $250K–$500K (or $500K + deposits only, or $3MM+ property, or Horizon credit card) |
| 3 | **Ultra / Premier Wealth** | $500K+ |

¹ "Investments" = GICs (min $1 AUM/A), mutual funds, DIY investing, and managed assets. Clients are assigned to the **highest qualifying tier** across all criteria.

**Why the AI features matter to tiering:** the tier framework explicitly lists "**AI agent for personalized guidance**", "**AI-assisted market research and portfolio analysis**", "**AI-powered capabilities**", and "**AI-enabled features**" as part of the digital experience across tiers — the AI capabilities in this repo are the concrete implementation of those promises.

## Client profitability context (why $250K & $500K are the thresholds)

| Segment | <$100K | $100K–250K | $250K–500K | $500K+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| # Clients | 115K | 19K | 6K | ~1.9K |
| Total assets | $2.7Bn | $2.9Bn | $2.1Bn | $1.8Bn |
| Avg assets/client | $23K | $153K | $350K | $961K |
| Avg rev/client (~86 bps) | ~$200 | ~$1,300 | ~$3,000 | ~$8,300 |

Value is concentrated above $250K; economics step up sharply at $500K.
